- **Coverage floor (PLAN, FR19 conventions).** Before hand-back the job runs `python scripts/ci.py contract-coverage` (the CI gate: `--cov-fail-under=80` over `dadaia_workspace`) on its tree and quotes the total line in its last commit body.

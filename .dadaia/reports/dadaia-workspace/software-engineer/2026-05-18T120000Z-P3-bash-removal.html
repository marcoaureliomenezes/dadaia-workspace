Summary

Phase P3 strips the Bash tool from two agent frontmatters (product-engineer and software-architect) and updates their body text to delegate all shell invocations to project-manager, which holds the orchestrator role and retains Bash.

Both removals are "documentation-truth alignment" as declared in the SPEC.
